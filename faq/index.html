Toronto Windshield Repair FAQ

Do you repair windshields?

No. This is an independent directory. The shops do the work.

Do you pass on my phone number or details?

No. When you tap Call, your phone dials the shop directly. We do not collect your details.

Why are some shops listed first?

Featured shops pay for placement and are labelled Featured. Everyone else is alphabetical.

Are the shops endorsed or checked for quality?

No. We confirm each shop's address, phone and stated services. We do not inspect work or endorse any shop.

Why don't you show reviews?

We only show information we can confirm. Check a shop's reviews on Google or elsewhere before booking.

Can my chip be repaired?

Often, if it is smaller than a loonie and not in front of the driver or camera. Chip repair guide.

Does my car need camera calibration?

If it has a camera behind the windshield and the glass is replaced, usually yes. Calibration guide.

Will insurance cover it?

It depends on your policy. Insurance guide. General information, not insurance advice.

Is it illegal to drive with a cracked windshield in Ontario?

Ontario's Highway Traffic Act (section 74) requires a clear view through the windshield. Whether a specific crack breaks that rule depends on its size and location.

I own a shop. How do I get listed?

Use the listing form. Basic listings are free.
