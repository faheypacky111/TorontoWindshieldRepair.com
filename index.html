Common questions

Do you repair windshields?

No. This is an independent directory of Toronto auto glass shops. Every phone number on this site rings the shop directly. The shop quotes and does the work.

How were these shops chosen?

We list Toronto and GTA auto glass shops whose address, phone and services we could confirm on the shop's own website or its chain's official website. A listing is not an endorsement. How the directory works.

Can my chip be repaired, or do I need a new windshield?

It depends on size, depth and location. A common guideline: chips smaller than a loonie and cracks shorter than about 15 cm (6 inches) are often repairable. Damage in front of the camera or in your direct line of sight usually means replacement. Chip repair guide.

Why are some shops listed first?

Featured shops pay for placement and are clearly labelled Featured. All other shops are listed alphabetically. Order is not a ranking of quality.

Will my insurance pay for it?

It depends on your policy. Glass damage is usually covered only under optional coverage, and deductibles vary. How glass claims generally work. General information, not insurance advice.
