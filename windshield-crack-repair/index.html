Windshield Crack Repair in Toronto

Short cracks can sometimes be repaired. Long or edge cracks usually need new glass. Here is how to tell.

See 5 shops that do this

Can a cracked windshield be repaired?

Sometimes. A short, clean crack can often be filled with resin. A long crack, a crack from the edge, or a crack in front of the driver usually means a new windshield.

Crack typeUsually
Under about 15 cm (6 in), away from the edgeOften repairable
Growing out of a small chip, still shortOften repairable if caught early
Starts at or near the edgeUsually replacement
Longer than about 15 cm (6 in)Usually replacement
In front of the camera or head-up display areaReplacement

Limits differ between shops. Some repair longer cracks than others.

How to measure your crack

  1. Park somewhere safe and turn the car off.
  2. Hold a ruler against the outside of the glass.
  3. Measure end to end in a straight line.
  4. Note where it starts: edge, middle, or in front of the driver.

Give the shop the length and location when you call. It speeds up the quote.

Why cracks get longer

Glass expands and shrinks with temperature. Cold mornings, hot defrost, sun on cold glass, potholes and slammed doors all add stress at the tip of a crack.

  • Use gentle defrost, not full heat on frozen glass.
  • Don't pour hot water on an icy windshield.
  • Avoid car washes until a shop has seen it.

Is it legal to drive with a cracked windshield in Ontario?

Ontario's Highway Traffic Act (section 74) requires a clear view through the windshield. A crack across the driver's view can be a problem under that rule.

Common questions

Can a 6-inch crack be repaired?

Sometimes. About 15 cm (6 inches) is a common limit, so it's a borderline case. Location matters as much as length.

My crack started at the edge. Can it be fixed?

Usually not. Many shops replace the windshield when a crack starts at or near the edge.
