About TorontoWindshieldRepair.com

TorontoWindshieldRepair.com is an independent directory of auto glass shops in Toronto. It helps drivers find a shop by area and service, and learn what to ask before they call.

We are not an auto glass shop and do not perform repairs. Every phone number on this site rings the shop directly.

How we make money

Shops can pay for a featured listing, which is clearly labelled. Basic listings are free.
