Windshield Replacement in Toronto

When the damage is too big, too deep or in your line of sight, the windshield gets replaced. Here is what to expect.

See 22 shops that do this

When a windshield needs replacing

  • The crack is long, usually more than about 15 cm (6 in).
  • The crack starts at the edge.
  • The damage is in front of the driver, the camera or the head-up display area.
  • The damage is deep, or there are several chips or cracks.

What happens during a replacement

  1. The old windshield is cut out and the frame is cleaned.
  2. A bead of urethane adhesive goes on the frame.
  3. The new windshield is set in place and the trim goes back on.
  4. If your car has a windshield camera, it is recalibrated. See ADAS calibration.

How long it takes

One Ontario auto glass shop puts a standard replacement at about 1 to 2 hours, with camera calibration adding another 1 to 2 hours on vehicles that need it.

Safe drive-away time

A windshield is held in by adhesive, not bolts. The adhesive needs time to get strong enough before you drive. It depends on the adhesive and the weather. Ask the shop for the safe drive-away time.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Does my vehicle need calibration? Is it in the price?
  • What type of glass will you install?
  • What is the safe drive-away time?
  • What warranty do you give?

Insurance: replacement is often handled through optional glass coverage, and a deductible may apply. How glass claims generally work.

General information, not legal or insurance advice. Coverage depends on your policy.

Common questions

Can I drive right after the windshield is replaced?

Not right away. Wait for the safe drive-away time the shop gives you.

Does my car need calibration after replacement?

If there is a camera behind the windshield, usually yes. More on calibration.
