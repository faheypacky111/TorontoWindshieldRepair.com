Windshield Chip Repair in Toronto

A stone chip is often fixable if it gets looked at before it spreads. Learn what shops check, then call one.

See 22 shops that do this

What chip repair is

A technician injects clear resin into the chip and cures it. The resin fills the break, restores strength to that spot and makes the damage much harder to see. The glass is not replaced, so the original factory seal stays in place.

Repair or replace?

Shops look at size, depth, location and how old the damage is. These are common guidelines, not fixed rules.

DamageUsually
Chip smaller than a loonieRepairable
Crack shorter than about 15 cm (6 in)Often repairable
Chip or crack in front of the camera or the head-up display areaReplacement
Damage halfway through the glass or deeperReplacement
Long crack, or a crack running from the edgeReplacement

Why fix it soon

Toronto's freeze-thaw swings, hot defrost on cold glass and potholes all stress the weak spot. That is how a small chip becomes a long crack that needs new glass.

  • Don't aim hot defrost straight at the chip on a cold morning.
  • Keep dirt and car-wash water out of it.
  • Close doors gently and take potholes slowly.

What to tell the shop

  1. Your vehicle's year, make and model.
  2. How big the chip is (smaller or bigger than a loonie).
  3. Where it is: driver's side, passenger side, or in front of the camera.
  4. How many chips there are.

Is it safe to drive with a chip?

A small chip outside your line of sight is usually not an immediate visibility problem, but it weakens the glass. Ontario's Highway Traffic Act (section 74) requires a clear view through the windshield. Whether a specific chip breaks that rule depends on its size and location.

Insurance: some policies cover chip repair, some don't. How glass claims generally work.

Common questions

Will the chip disappear after repair?

Usually not completely. The resin restores strength and makes the chip much less visible, but a small mark often remains.

Can a chip in front of the windshield camera be repaired?

Generally no. Safelite's guidance is that damage in front of the forward-facing camera or inside the head-up display area means replacement, which usually needs camera calibration too.
