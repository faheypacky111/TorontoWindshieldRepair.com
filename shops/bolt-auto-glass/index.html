What to ask when you call

  1. Can you repair this, or does it need a new windshield? (Have the size and location of the damage ready.)
  2. What is the total price, including any camera calibration?
  3. How long will it take, and what is the safe drive-away time?
  4. What warranty do you give on the work?
  5. Will you deal with my insurer, or do I pay and claim myself?
