Driving in Mississauga

Highways 401, 403, 410, 427 and the QEW all run through Mississauga, and the area around Pearson Airport carries heavy truck traffic. Stones from trucks and gravel from construction are common causes of chips.

Several Mississauga shops listed here work out of industrial units, so call ahead to book a time rather than walking in.

Which damage needs what

A chip smaller than a loonie is often repairable. A crack longer than about 15 cm (6 in), a crack from the edge, or damage in front of the driver or the windshield camera usually means replacement. Read the chip, crack and replacement guides.
