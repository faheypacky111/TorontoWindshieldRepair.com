Windshield Insurance Claims in Toronto

Whether your windshield is covered depends on your policy. Here is how glass coverage generally works in Ontario.

See 5 shops that do this

General information only. Not insurance advice. Your insurer has the final answer.

Which coverage pays for glass

In Ontario, windshield damage from stones, hail or vandalism is usually covered only if your policy includes optional physical damage coverage, such as comprehensive, specified perils or all perils. With only the mandatory coverages, a stone chip is generally not covered.

Deductibles

Some policies waive the deductible for small chip repairs. For a full replacement, a deductible usually applies.

Limited glass coverage (OPCF 13C)

Some Ontario policies include OPCF 13C, which limits glass coverage in exchange for a lower premium. Check your policy documents for "13C".

Can a claim affect my insurance?

It can. Several glass claims may lead your insurer to change your glass coverage or deductible.

Steps to check your coverage

  1. Find your policy documents or your insurer's app.
  2. Look for comprehensive, specified perils or all perils coverage.
  3. Look for your deductible and any "13C" endorsement.
  4. Call your insurer: "Is windshield repair or replacement covered, and what is my deductible?"
  5. If your car has a windshield camera, ask whether calibration is covered too.

We do not promise any repair will be covered. Shops in the list say on their own websites that they help with insurance claims; we have not verified how they handle claims or deductibles. Ask the shop and your insurer.

Common questions

Is a windshield covered without comprehensive insurance?

Generally not for stone chips. Glass is usually covered under optional coverage. Check your policy.
