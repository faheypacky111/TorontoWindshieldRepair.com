Mobile Windshield Repair in Toronto

Some shops send a technician to your home or work. Here is when that works and when you'll need to visit the shop.

See 9 shops that do this

What mobile service means

A technician comes to where your car is parked and does the repair or replacement there.

When a shop visit may be needed instead

  • Weather: rain, extreme cold and high wind can stop an outdoor job.
  • Space: the technician needs room around the vehicle.
  • Calibration: some vehicles need camera calibration in a shop with special targets.

Before the appointment

  1. Pick a spot with room on all sides of the car.
  2. Check your condo or work lot allows vehicle work.
  3. Have your keys available.
  4. Ask the shop what happens if it rains.

General information, not legal or insurance advice. Coverage depends on your policy.

Common questions

Does mobile service cost more?

It depends on the shop. Ask when you call.

Can a windshield be replaced outside in winter?

Sometimes, but cold slows the adhesive. The shop may reschedule or ask for a covered spot.
